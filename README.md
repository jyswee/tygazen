# TYGAZEN™

**One identity. Drawn in 2D or 3D. Yours across every world it travels to.**

A tygazen is a character you build once and keep. Not a profile picture, and not
a different avatar per game — one identity, assembled from a shared part library,
that can be drawn as a pixel person on the web and as a 3D character in a world,
and is recognisably the same person in both.

## What it is

- **Build a face before you sign up.** The character comes first and the account
  second. Choose hair, eyes, mouth, clothing and the rest from a shared library,
  reroll until it is yours, and claim it afterwards.
- **One identity, several representations.** The same character can be rendered
  flat for a feed or a leaderboard, and as a 3D body for a world. Both are drawn
  from the same part set, so they are the same person rather than two lookalikes.
- **It travels.** A tygazen is bound to you, not to a title. A game that adopts
  the pattern gets your existing character on day one instead of asking you to
  make another.
- **Change it anywhere, it changes everywhere.** Restyle your face once and every
  surface that shows you follows, with no re-pick and nothing to re-upload.
- **Yourself, or someone else.** A tygazen can be close to who you actually are,
  or a deliberate alter ego you keep for a world and nowhere else. Both are
  first-class, and nothing on the page tells anyone which you chose.

## Where you will meet one

Tygazens live in the Tyga community and travel into the games that adopt them.
They are the inhabitants of **Tygaria**, a world built around them.

**https://tygazen.com**

## For studios

Tygazen is offered to game studios as an identity service: your players build a
character in a page you embed, you read the result back and bind it to the account
you create, and you render it from flat images or from the part set directly. One
avatar per person, shared across every game on the network — no asset pipeline and
no account required before the pick.
